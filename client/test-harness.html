pay.js test harness

Drives client/pay.js against the live production endpoint with your own wallet.

This spends real money. Each paid call transfers $0.02 USDC on Base from your wallet to the payout wallet 0xd4ec…6bc9. If that payout wallet is yours, the money goes from one of your pockets to another and the round trip costs you nothing — the facilitator pays the gas, not you. Nothing here ever sees a private key: your wallet signs, this page relays.
