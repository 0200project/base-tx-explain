Engagements

An independent third party who tells you what a transaction actually did — and what it could not check.

We examine your own on-chain activity and hand you a written finding with the raw evidence attached, in a form you can reproduce yourself. Scoped work, agreed before it starts. Not a licence, and not a seat.

Settlement forensics

What a transaction or a set of them actually moved, which fields were controlled by the transaction’s author, and which checks could not be completed against it.

Attribution audit

For a set of addresses, what can be established from the chain and what cannot. The second list is the one most reports leave out.

Reconciliation

Your own record of what happened, compared against what the chain shows, with every difference itemised and evidenced.

What this is not

You receive JSON and a written artifact. Not a signed attestation, and not an opinion you can hand to a regulator. It is our reading of public chain data with the evidence attached so you can check every step of it yourself — which is the part that makes it worth having, and also the limit of what it is.
