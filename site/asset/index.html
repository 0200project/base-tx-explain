Native asset

What exists, what is being built, and what is still a hypothesis

State as of 2026-09-02. Every claim on this page is labelled with which of those three it is.

This page exists so a reader can tell, without asking us, which of our claims describe something that runs today and which describe an intention. Those are different kinds of statement and mixing them is how a roadmap starts reading like a product.

Three labels are used throughout, and nothing appears without one:

  • CURRENT — exists, runs, and can be checked right now.
  • PROSPECTIVE — being built deliberately. Carries a review date. On that date it becomes CURRENT with evidence, or is marked not pursued. It does not sit here indefinitely.
  • UNPROVEN — a hypothesis we hold and have not demonstrated. May change or disappear.

CURRENT — what exists today

There is no canonical 0200 native asset. It has not been deployed. It has no contract address, no name, no ticker, and no supply. Nothing on this page describes a token you can hold, and there is nothing to buy.

One product runs: base-transaction-decoder. It explains any Base mainnet transaction as strict JSON, is priced in USDC, and requires no token to use. It has never accepted any token as payment.

One token exists and is being retired: the Genesis token, 0xC626A027A0Fa57A9424Df1173b51d80d26B44FA6. As of this writing its supply still sits in the deployer’s wallet and the retirement transactions have not been executed. Its full history, the risk disclosure, and the retirement notice are published separately and are not summarised here.

Control, stated plainly because it is the field that matters most: every asset 0200project holds is controlled by a single private key held by one person. There is no multisig, no timelock, and no on-chain constraint. Who can move these tomorrow, and what stops them? Today the answer is: one person, and nothing.

PROSPECTIVE — being built, not yet real

Each item below is an intention. None of it is deployed. None of the numbers exist yet, so none are published here — a figure describing an undeployed contract is not a fact about anything.

  • A canonical native asset. Decided in principle. Not designed, not named, not deployed.
  • A rate-limited release mechanism. The design intent is that no holder, including us, can move more than a fixed amount in any 30-day window, enforced by code that nobody can alter. Until it is deployed and its parameters are readable on-chain, this is an intention and not a bound. When it exists, this page will publish the maximum that can reach a market in any 30-day window, what that amount does to the liquidity it would land in, and the address of any portion held outside the mechanism — which would have no ceiling at all.
  • Custody architecture. Not designed. The single-key position described above is the current state and changing it is a prerequisite, not a follow-up.
  • Allocation. Not decided. No allocation percentages are published anywhere by 0200project, and any that appear elsewhere are not ours.

Review date for every item in this section: 2026-12-02. On that date each becomes CURRENT with evidence, or is marked not pursued, and this page says which.

UNPROVEN — hypotheses

The thesis. That deterministic, machine-verifiable settlement state is useful infrastructure for autonomous commerce. We have verified one mechanism on-chain and built nothing on it. It may not be useful. It may not be needed.

That the asset is necessary at all. We established separately that the protocol functions without a native asset, and that finding stands. The asset exists because the founder judged it strategically necessary; it is not presented as technically required. This section exists so a reader does not have to infer which of those two things is true.

What we do not do

0200project does not market a native asset on the basis of expected price appreciation. We publish no market capitalisation, no supply-multiplied-by-price figure, and no return expectation of any kind. Where third-party tools display such numbers for tokens associated with us, they are computed by those tools and not by us.

This page is not overwritten. When a claim changes tier or turns out to be wrong, the correction is added with a date and the previous text stays visible, so that a reader in five years can see what we claimed in 2026 rather than only what we claim then.
